How to Order

By tr0utsw0rld

Every bag is crocheted by hand, so yours can be made exactly how you picture it — your shape, your colors, your little details.

  1. Pick a style. A handbag, a shoulder bag, a mini, a tote — or something new entirely.
  2. Send your palette. Upload any photo with colors you love — a sunset, an outfit, a Pinterest screenshot.
  3. Share your inspo. Photos, a link, or a few words about the vibe, plus any customizations.
  4. I review your request. I'll look it over to see if I can achieve your vision. If I can't, we'll work together to create something based on my capabilities.

Then I'll reach out on Instagram with ideas, pricing and timing.
